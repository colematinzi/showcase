My Projects

Here you'll find an outline of my most recent and
meaningful writing projects, including internal
documentation, client-facing materials, long-form
manuals, and instructional content.

Internal Knowledge Base Articles — Zwift

At Zwift, I maintained and expanded the company’s internal knowledge base of over 500 articles used by 150+ Community Support agents. I created and updated documentation, chat shortcuts, and process guides using MadCap Flare, Google Workspace, Kustomer, and AWS. Collaborating closely with subject matter experts, I filled information gaps and ensured documentation kept pace with new product launches and feature updates. I also reviewed draft articles for clarity, consistency, and accuracy, helping streamline onboarding and improve efficiency across the support organization.

Client-Facing Documentation — Folia Materials

At Folia Materials, I contributed to the company’s early-stage technical and marketing documentation, helping communicate complex materials science concepts to non-technical audiences. I collaborated with subject matter experts across departments to develop clear, accurate FAQ content for the company website and reviewed investor-facing slide decks for clarity, tone, and consistency. I also created written overviews and explanatory materials about the company’s recyclable susceptor technology, which were used at conventions and trade shows to engage potential partners and investors.

“Microsoft Word Manual” — Technical Writing Course Project

Created as part of my technical writing coursework, this 28-page manual guides students through Microsoft Word’s core features for drafting and formatting a thesis. Designed for users transitioning from Google Docs, it includes step-by-step instructions and annotated visuals covering formatting, footnotes, revision tools, and PDF export.
